Before you buy: what decides it

Adhesive mounts depend on the surface and the load; a full roll pulling off a painted cabinet is the usual regret.

Three questions to answer first

  1. What is under your cabinet: bare wood, painted MDF, or a glossy laminate?
  2. How wide is the roll you actually buy, and will it clear the backsplash?
  3. Would you rather drill once than trust adhesive?
