Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

DIY HOME upgrades participates in the Amazon Associates program. Some of the product links on this site are outbound affiliate links. When you click one and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Tracking ID referenced in this project: shopnow047c-20.

Placement: this disclosure strip appears under the masthead on every page, including the landing page, and is repeated in the footer on every page — so it is above the fold wherever a reader enters.

Link marking: every outbound product link on this site is marked rel="sponsored" and opens in a new tab. No outbound product link is presented as an editorial endorsement without this marking.

FTC endorsement context

We do not claim that any product is popular, best-selling, or superior. Guide titles are working titles only. Where compensation could apply, the relationship is disclosed near the link. Reviews, ratings, pricing and commission potential shown anywhere on this site must be confirmed against an official Amazon source before display; unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged unverified.

Amazon Associates policy checks (publishing workflow gate)

  • The Associates statement remains visible on every page (above the fold + footer).
  • Disclosure placement and endorsement context are reviewed against the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and FTC guidance before publishing.
  • Product-intent confirmation records (intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date, source; observed redirect target in a separate field) are maintained per link and never merge redirect targets with official listing URLs.
  • Short-link compliance: supplied short links are not claimed invalid; their exact compliance status is unresolved. They are kept as submitted, marked sponsored, pending validation as Amazon Special Links in the enrolled Associates workflow — one link per product tested and confirmed before scaling.

This statement is a project artifact. For binding compliance questions, consult the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and FTC guidance directly (links below).
