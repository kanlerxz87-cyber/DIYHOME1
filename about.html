Executive summary

The Practical Home launches with four kitchen buying guides. The submitted URLs were observed landing on wallpaper tools and kitchen storage, not air fryers. Product intent and attribution remain unconfirmed, so no affiliate offers are live. The lead image is illustrative, not a verified product photo.

Publishing plan

Review search queries and landing pages monthly; aim for one new guide each month when measured queries and reliable sources justify it. Review seasonal links before major shopping periods. Each new guide goes in the sitemap and RSS on build. RSS is the reader follow option; this site does not collect email addresses.

Publishing gate

The build blocks incomplete offer records; it does not authenticate evidence. Do not scrape listings or use restricted Program Content for AI without explicit authorization.
