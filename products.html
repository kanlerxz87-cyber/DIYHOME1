Notes on the confirmation record

  • For each submitted link, the product-intent confirmation record stores: intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date, and source. Observed redirect targets are kept in a separate field and are never merged with the official listing URL (Amazon's ASIN definition provides a checkable identifier).
  • Duplicate submission: https://link.amazon/B03QDuGdo was submitted twice; it is treated as one product entry (rows 2 above covers it; the duplicate is noted, not duplicated).
  • ASIN format irregularity: the path segments in the submitted short links (B0hmGG4m2, B03QDuGdo, B0hpD2JGQ) are 9 characters; Amazon ASINs are 10 characters, so these segments are not asserted to be ASINs. Intended ASIN is left blank.
  • Short-link compliance: per project guidance, the supplied short links are not claimed invalid; their exact compliance status is unresolved. They are kept as submitted, marked rel="sponsored", pending validation as Amazon Special Links in the enrolled Associates workflow.
  • Verification limit: these links and any Amazon product details (title, price, reviews, commission potential) cannot be resolved from this sandbox, because outbound network access is limited to GitHub/npm/PyPI and Amazon is not reachable. All rows are therefore flagged unverified until confirmed against an official Amazon source.
