What should you ask before buying a multi-function appliance?

The Practical Home editorial desk · November 20, 2026

Choose the functions you would use weekly and check what each requires.

Name two meals you make regularly and find the manual’s instructions for those exact modes. Check which accessories come in the box and where you would store them. If your current appliance already handles one meal well, ask whether replacing it solves a problem or only adds another setting to learn.

One safety check before buying

USDA advises against overcrowding an air fryer because it can restrict airflow and prevent proper cooking. Check food with a thermometer. Read USDA food-safety guidance.
