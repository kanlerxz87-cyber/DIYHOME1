Privacy by design

The optional collector stores daily aggregate counts by allowlisted page and product only. No cookies, local storage, IP addresses, user agents, full URLs, search strings or referrers are stored by application code. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. There is no cross-device or individual attribution.

Orders are a separate source

An outbound click is not a sale. Eligible sessions and reported orders must come from authorized first-party reports with report references and dates. The browser never emits a purchase event. No conversion rate can be computed until an appropriate, compatible denominator and reporting window exist.
