Privacy and measurement

First-party analytics collector: Not configured. When enabled, the site sends aggregate events for page views, guide views, RSS link clicks, and affiliate link clicks. Reported orders are an authorized report import, never a browser event or a purchase inferred from a click.

The collector stores day, event name, page path and count only. The site does not set analytics cookies, store visitor IDs, or send full referrer or query strings. The hosting provider may independently process connection data.
