The kitchen edit / Buying guide

Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

A starting point for matching the appliance to the way you cook.

These are general questions, not a product test or a recommendation. Check the manufacturer’s current instructions for any model you consider.

Start with the food you cook most often and the space you can spare. The shape of an appliance can matter as much as its stated capacity.

Think about the way you cook

  • Would you rather pull out a basket or use shelves and trays?
  • What size and shape of food do you expect to make? Check the usable interior dimensions, not just the exterior.
  • Can you comfortably reach the controls, open the door or drawer, and remove hot food?

Before you decide

Compare the manufacturer's dimensions, cleaning instructions, accessories and electrical requirements. Leave the ventilation clearance specified in its manual. Features vary by model; check the listing and manual rather than assuming they are included.
