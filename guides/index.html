Buying guides, written as questions

One guide a week for the first month, each tied to a category that has a verified product. They are questions because the useful part of a buying decision is usually a measurement, not a ranking.

What these guides do not claim

Four guides, one per week of the first month. Every guide is question-shaped and tied to a category that actually has a verified product in data/products.json. No search-volume, popularity or superiority claims are made anywhere: no keyword tool with first-party or licensed data was available to this session.
