A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Look beyond the number on the box and think about what you actually make.

These are general questions, not a product test or a recommendation. Check the manufacturer’s current instructions for any model you consider.

A capacity figure is a starting point, not a guarantee that a particular meal will fit. Consider your own portions, cookware and counter space.

Bring these questions to the listing

  • What are the usable interior dimensions, and what shape is the cooking area?
  • Will the foods you prepare most often fit without crowding?
  • Does the exterior fit your space with the clearance required by the manufacturer?
  • What does the manual say about quantities and recommended loading?

Compare like with like: a volume on one listing may not describe the same usable shape as another.
