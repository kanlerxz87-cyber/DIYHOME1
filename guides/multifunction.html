The kitchen edit / Buying guide

What should you ask before buying a multi-function appliance?

Consider the functions you will use, and the space you have to give.

These are general questions, not a product test or a recommendation. Check the manufacturer’s current instructions for any model you consider.

A long feature list is not the same thing as a good fit. Begin with the tasks you actually do, then check whether the appliance supports them in a way that works for you.

A practical shortlist

  • Which functions will you use regularly, and which might duplicate something you own?
  • Are the necessary accessories included or sold separately?
  • How much room does it need in use and in storage?
  • What do the manufacturer's instructions say about cleaning, power and ventilation?

Compare the model's official manual and listing before purchasing. This guide does not rank or recommend a specific product.
