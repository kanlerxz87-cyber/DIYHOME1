The kitchen edit / Buying guide

How do cleanup and counter space trade off?

A few practical questions to ask before making room for something new.

These are general questions, not a product test or a recommendation. Check the manufacturer’s current instructions for any model you consider.

It is easy to focus on features and forget the everyday routine. Before choosing an appliance, picture where it will live and what cleanup looks like after dinner.

Check the details

  • Measure the counter and the appliance, including room to open doors or drawers.
  • Check the manual for ventilation clearance and placement requirements.
  • Which parts come out, and what does the manufacturer say about washing them?
  • Can the parts fit in your sink or dishwasher if the manufacturer permits it?

Ease of cleaning is personal; inspect the parts and instructions rather than assuming one design is always easier.
