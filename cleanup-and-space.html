How much cleanup and counter space should you plan for?

The Practical Home editorial desk · November 20, 2026

Picture the appliance with the drawer or door open, and the parts in your sink.

Measure the counter, including front clearance, and check the manual for the manufacturer’s ventilation requirements. List the parts you expect to wash after a meal and verify which, if any, the manual says are dishwasher-safe. Before shopping, decide where the hot basket or tray can safely land while you serve.

One safety check before buying

USDA advises against overcrowding an air fryer because it can restrict airflow and prevent proper cooking. Check food with a thermometer. Read USDA food-safety guidance.
