Editorial cadence

Review official evidence each Monday. Publish at most one evidence-backed guide on Tuesday, then add it automatically to the feed and sitemap through the build. Review measured queries and authorized reports monthly before choosing the next topic. This is a proposed process, not a claim that a staffed publishing schedule exists.

Release boundary

Unconfirmed products may appear as transparent observed listings, never as endorsed purchases. The affiliate gate requires an intended ASIN, official confirmation, date, source, a matching tagged Special Link and enrolled-report confirmation. Program content ingestion additionally needs an API-policy review. Current listings do not pass that gate.
