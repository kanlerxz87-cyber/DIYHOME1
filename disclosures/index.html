Affiliate disclosure

DIY Home Upgrades is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Every outbound link to Amazon on this site is a sponsored affiliate link and carries the tracking ID shopnow047c-20.

The tracking ID used on this site is shopnow047c-20. It was supplied by the site owner. This site does not claim that the ID is enrolled or active in an Associates account: that confirmation requires signing in to Associates Central and is flagged as F01 and F12.

Where the disclosure appears

  • A strip directly under the masthead on every page, above the content, marked Sponsored, with a link to this page.
  • Repeated in the footer of every page, in the affiliate paragraph.
  • On the link itself: every outbound Amazon link is labelled Sponsored and carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".

FTC guidance asks for a disclosure that is hard to miss, placed with the endorsement rather than only on an about page, in simple language such as “advertisement”, “ad” or “sponsored”. That is why the strip sits under the masthead rather than in a footer-only position, and why the word used is Sponsored. S03

Amazon’s program terms also require Associates to satisfy applicable disclosure law and to disclose cookies, pixels and third-party data collection accurately. This site sets no cookies and makes no third-party requests, which is stated on the measurement page. S01

How links are built

Outbound links go to https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20. Amazon’s Participation Requirements require the Associates tag to appear as a parameter in the link URL. S01

No redirector, cloaker or link shortener sits in front of these links, and clicks are not intercepted: a click is recorded and the browser navigates straight to Amazon. Amazon’s policies prohibit obscuring the URL containing Special Links, and prohibit shortening links in a way that makes it unclear you are linking to an Amazon Site. S01

The three short links supplied by the site owner are published only as provenance records on the product pages and in the ledger, labelled as unvalidated. They are not used as the call to action. The reasons are on F01.
