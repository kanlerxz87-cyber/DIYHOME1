Adhesive or screw-mounted: what should you compare?

A buying checklist for your paper towel holder.

By DIY HOME Editorial · Published November 20, 2026 · Editorial checklist · No hands-on testing

01. Choose a location first

Where would the roll be easiest to reach? Measure the proposed space with a roll in place, allowing room to replace it. Look for cabinet doors, handles and other objects that could get in the way.

02. Ask about the surface

Which surfaces does the manufacturer explicitly support for the adhesive? What preparation and curing instructions apply? If you rent, check the lease before attaching anything. Do not treat adhesive mounting as a guarantee of damage-free removal.

03. Check the supplied hardware

Does the exact variant include adhesive, screws or both? If drilling, follow the installation instructions and check the mounting surface before proceeding. This guide does not establish that either method is suitable for your home.

04. Read the actual listing

The observed destination is an under-cabinet paper towel holder. Its submitted product intent remains unconfirmed. Check the current variant and instructions directly before purchase.
