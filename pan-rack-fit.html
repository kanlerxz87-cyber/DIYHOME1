Will a pan organizer actually fit your cabinet?

Measure the space you have, not the space you hope for.

By DIY HOME Editorial · Published November 20, 2026 · Editorial checklist · No hands-on testing

01. Measure the opening and the interior

Record the usable cabinet width, depth and height as well as the door opening. Where are the hinges, shelves and plumbing? A useful comparison starts with your actual cabinet, not a photograph.

02. Count handles and lids too

List the cookware you want to store. Measure each piece including handles and lids. Ask the manufacturer about allowed cookware size and load limits; do not infer them from an illustration.

03. Compare arrangements

Would a horizontal or vertical arrangement let you take out the pan you use most? Sketch the arrangement and allow space to lift items out. Confirm the exact rack variant supports the arrangement in its instructions.
