Product-intent record and campaign ledger

Two records that stay separate: what a link was supposed to point at, and what it actually returned. Below them, a campaign ledger with a defined schema and no rows, because no first-party report has been obtained.

1. Product-intent confirmation record

For each submitted link: the intended ASIN, the official listing URL, the confirmation date and the source, with the observed redirect target kept in its own field. The two are never merged, because a redirect target is an observation and an intended ASIN is a claim.

Published from data/product-intents.csv. Blank intended ASIN means the short link carries no ASIN, not that none exists.

2. Campaign ledger

Empty by design. A row is added only from an authorised first-party report, with the timestamp, the source, the channel, eligible sessions, clicks and reported orders. Blank means “not obtained”; it never means zero. No conversion score, revenue forecast or commission percentage is presented anywhere on this site, because the rate card and reports both require a sign-in this build did not have.
